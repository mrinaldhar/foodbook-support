# Foodbook privacy policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Foodbook is a kitchen inventory, recipe and shopping-list app.

## What stays on your device

Inventory, shopping lists, preferences, cooking history and add-speed metrics are stored locally on your device using Apple SwiftData. The current build does not upload them.

## Camera and photos

- Camera access is used only to scan barcodes. A manual barcode fallback works without it.
- Receipt photos are read on-device with Apple Vision. Receipt images are not uploaded.
- A feedback screenshot is uploaded only when you tap **Send feedback**.

## Feedback

If you send feedback, the app sends your summary, details, expected behaviour, attached screenshot, app version/build, device model, iOS version and locale. The report is stored in our Supabase project and a GitHub issue is created for the team. TestFlight feedback is collected by Apple and shown to us in App Store Connect.

## What we do not do

- No advertising or cross-app tracking.
- No third-party analytics SDK in this build.
- No sale of personal data.
- No account or email is required to use the app.

## Retention and deletion

Feedback is kept while useful for fixing the product. Email **webmaster.mrinal@gmail.com** to ask for a report to be deleted.
